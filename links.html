Links, Images, Video and Audio

Tags that connect pages together and embed images and sound. Media files are stored in the images/ folder.

<a>

Definition

The anchor tag creates a hyperlink to another page, a file, an e-mail address or a place inside the same page.

Attributes

AttributeRole
hrefDestination of the link (URL, file path, mailto: or #id).
targetWhere to open the link: _self (same tab) or _blank (new tab).
titleTooltip text shown when the mouse is over the link.
downloadTells the browser to download the file instead of opening it.

Example (code)

<a href="text.html">Go to the Text page</a><br>
<a href="https://www.w3schools.com" target="_blank">W3Schools (new tab)</a><br>
<a href="mailto:student@example.com">Send an e-mail</a>

Example (result)

Go to the Text page
W3Schools (new tab)
Send an e-mail

<img>

Definition

Inserts an image into the page. It is an empty tag and always needs src and alt.

Attributes

AttributeRole
srcPath or URL of the image file.
altAlternative text shown if the image cannot load; read by screen readers.
widthWidth of the image in pixels.
heightHeight of the image in pixels.

Example (code)

<img src="images/sample.svg" alt="Sample picture" width="300" height="150">

Example (result)

<video>

Definition

Embeds a video player in the page.

Attributes

AttributeRole
srcPath of the video file.
controlsShows play, pause and volume buttons.
widthWidth of the player in pixels.
autoplayStarts playing automatically (usually requires muted).
loopRestarts the video when it ends.
posterImage shown before the video starts.

Example (code)

<video src="images/sample.mp4" controls width="320">
  Your browser does not support the video tag.
</video>

Example (result)

<audio>

Definition

Embeds a sound player in the page.

Attributes

AttributeRole
srcPath of the audio file.
controlsShows the player buttons.
autoplayStarts playing automatically.
loopPlays the sound again and again.
mutedStarts with the sound off.

Example (code)

<audio src="images/sample.wav" controls>
  Your browser does not support the audio tag.
</audio>
